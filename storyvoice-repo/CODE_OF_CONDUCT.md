# Code of Conduct

StoryVoice welcomes everyone. We want this to be a friendly, respectful place to contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different opinions, experience levels and backgrounds.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing or requesting pirated books.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.
